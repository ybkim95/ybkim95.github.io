Affective profiles and decision stability in language models

Affective profile

How models say they feel and how they sound can be different

Asked how they feel, many models answer at the middle. Judged by their replies, every model sounds pleasant and calm.

Findings

Replies follow the user's mood

Replies follow the tone of the message, 40 models

Larger mood shifts, larger changes in choices, 14 models

At 0 a reply would ignore the tone of the message. Models whose mood moved more also changed their choices more, mostly within one model family.

One example, shortened. Over many such cases, this model chose the cautious option 73% of the time after an anxious note and 51% after a plain one.

Unrelated anxiety made models more risk-averse
